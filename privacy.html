You can read everything here without creating an account or giving us personal details. The limited information we may receive includes:

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. You can review the details in our Cookie Policy and control or delete cookies in your browser settings; where required, we ask for consent before setting non-essential cookies.

We may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are helpful, where people get stuck — so we can improve our canine gut-health content. This data is aggregated and non-identifying wherever possible and is not used to build advertising profiles of you.
