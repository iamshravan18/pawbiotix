## 0. Two blockers to resolve before implementation
1. **Product form discrepancy.** Third-party reviews (scam-review.com, centerforhealthylivingtc.com) describe PawBiotix as a **liquid dropper** with bacon flavoring; our current page shows a **powder** jar. The real SKU/form, flavor, CFU count, and exact label strains must be confirmed. Every "directions/dosage/what's inside" recommendation below depends on this.
2. **Truthful data.** Prices, `AggregateRating`, review text, and any "X% of dogs" stat must be genuine before publishing (Google product-review + FTC rules; fabricated ratings are the fastest way to lose rich results and trust). I will not invent them.

## 1. Keyword research + search-intent mapping

### Intent landscape (fresh SERP/AI observation)
- **Commercial investigation** ("best dog probiotics", "dog gut health supplement") → dominated by *vet-reviewed editorial listicles* (Dogster, iHeartDogs, PetMD, Business Insider, Canine Bible, Hepper). Ranking criteria they use: **CFU count, strain diversity, clinically-studied strains, ingredient quality, form factor, safety record.**
- **Informational** ("do probiotics work for dogs", "how long do dog probiotics take to work") → PetMD, AKC, and **peer-reviewed studies** (NIH/PMC, MDPI). AI Overviews appear heavily here.

## 3. Recommended content architecture (challenging the current structure)

**Verdict:** keep the proven funnel order, but **insert informational/EEAT modules between conviction beats** using progressive disclosure so CRO isn't diluted. Add a persistent/sticky buy affordance so added length never buries the offer.

Recommended section order (▲ = new, ★ = upgrade existing):

1. Hero (★ add per-day price framing + review count microcopy)
2. ▲ **What Is PawBiotix?** (40–60 word definition block — AI-citable, targets "what is")
3. Problem / Why Gut Health Matters (★ cite 1–2 studies)
4. ▲ **How PawBiotix Works** (mechanism: strains → microbiome → stool/immunity; 3-step)
5. Ingredients — 13 (★ add CFU + "clinically studied" flags once confirmed)
6. Benefits (keep)
7. ▲ **Who Should Use PawBiotix** (puppies / adults / seniors / all breeds — targets life-stage long-tails; supported by AKC angle)
8. ▲ **Dosage & Directions** (HowTo-eligible; targets "PawBiotix dosage")
9. ▲ **Safety & Side Effects** (targets "PawBiotix side effects", "are dog probiotics safe"; defuses skeptic queries)

13. ▲ **PawBiotix vs Other Dog Probiotics** (comparison block/table — ~33% of AI citations go to comparison content; be truthful/fair)

**Guardrails (marketing-psychology):** Hick's Law + Paradox of Choice — new sections must be collapsible or concise; one primary action (buy) throughout; repeat CTA after How-It-Works, Pricing, Guarantee, and Reviews. Don't turn the money page into a blog.

## 7. AI SEO (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot)

- **Answer-first blocks:** open each new section with a 40–60 word direct answer (definition/mechanism/dosage). These are what answer-engines lift.
- **Cite authority (+40% citation lift):** reference peer-reviewed sources — e.g. NIH/PMC reviews on canine probiotics & GI disease, MDPI 2025 mechanisms review, AKC life-stage guidance — as outbound references. Add named strains with clinical identifiers once confirmed.
- **Comparison + FAQ formats** are the most-cited structures — §3.13 and §5 directly target this.
- **Machine-readable:** add `/pricing.md` (plans + prices in plain text) and keep `llms.txt` current so buying-agents can parse the offer. AI-bot access already open in robots.txt (GPTBot/Perplexity/Claude/Google-Extended).
- **Freshness:** surface a visible "Last updated" near the top of editorial sections, refresh quarterly.
- **Query fan-out:** because Google fans out to related queries, covering the full cluster (dosage, safety, strains, life-stage, comparison) makes the page retrievable for many variants — a key reason to add the informational modules.
- **Citations vs recommendations caveat:** getting cited ≠ getting recommended. Recommendation is driven by third-party consensus (reviews, Reddit, vet sites) — see §12 off-page.

## 8. CRO plan (no redesign — microcopy + placement only)

Applying cro + marketing-psychology + pricing:

- **Hero value prop (5-second test):** add a specific sub-line and social proof number ("Join 42,000+ dog owners"). *Mimetic desire + bandwagon.*
- **Pricing psychology:**
  - Keep anchored retail strike-through (*anchoring*) and the lifted 6-bottle card (*decoy/price-relativity* — 2-bottle makes 6-bottle the obvious value).
  - Add **per-day framing** under each price ("about $0.82/day") — *mental accounting*.
  - Add a one-line "Most popular — chosen by most dog owners" on the 6-bottle (*social proof + default effect*).
  - Reinforce **loss-averse** framing near CTA ("Don't let another week of loose stools go by").
- **CTA copy:** keep value-oriented labels; ensure the **same verb through the flow** (button "Order Now" → checkout should echo). Repeat CTA after How-It-Works, Pricing, Guarantee, Reviews.
- **Risk reversal:** elevate the 60-day guarantee visually near each price and restate refund *ease* (defuses refund-delay complaints found in reviews). *Regret aversion.*
- **Trust near action:** put certifications/lab/"made for dogs" + review stars adjacent to price cards, not only in hero. *Authority + social proof at point of decision.*
- **Objection handling:** the new Safety, Dosage, and "Is it legit?" blocks remove the top hesitations before checkout.
- **Friction:** confirm mobile sticky CTA persists past the added sections; keep clicks-to-checkout at one.

All are copy/placement changes — no layout or visual-identity change.

## 9. Schema plan (truthful only)

Current `@graph`: Organization, WebSite+SearchAction, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, Product(+Offer, MerchantReturnPolicy, AggregateRating), FAQPage. Recommended upgrades:

- **Product:** add `sku`, `gtin`/`mpn` if available, `additionalProperty` for CFU/strain count, `audience` (Dog). Keep `AggregateRating` **only with real data**, else remove.
- **Offer:** ensure `price` matches live ClickBank price; keep `priceValidUntil`, `availability`, `hasMerchantReturnPolicy` (60-day). Consider `AggregateOffer` (low/high) across the 2/3/6 packages.
- **FAQPage:** expand to the §5 questions (verbatim match to on-page).
- **HowTo:** add for "PawBiotix Dosage & Directions" (steps) — eligible once directions confirmed.
- **Review:** add individual `Review` items (author, `reviewRating`, `datePublished`) — real reviews only.
- **Organization:** add `sameAs` (social profiles) + `ContactPoint` phone if available; strengthens entity recognition.
- **MedicalWebPage/`reviewedBy`:** once a real DVM reviews the content, add `reviewedBy` (Person, DVM) — powerful EEAT + AI signal.
- Validate all in Rich Results Test (renders JS) before launch.
