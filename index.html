Probiotics 101

What Are Dog Probiotics?

Dog probiotics are supplements containing live, beneficial bacteria that support a dog's gut. Given daily, they help maintain a balanced gut microbiome, aid digestion, and support the immune activity that lives largely in the digestive tract. Prebiotics are the plant fibers that feed those bacteria.

Probiotics

Live, beneficial bacteria (commonly Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium species) that help balance the gut and support healthy digestion.

Prebiotics

Plant fibers such as inulin that act as food for beneficial bacteria, helping them establish and flourish.

Why canine-specific?

A dog's digestive system differs from a human's, so vets generally favor probiotics formulated for dogs over human products.

The Canine Microbiome

Understanding Dog Gut Health

A dog's gut hosts trillions of microbes — the microbiome — and a large share of the immune system resides there. When that balance is disrupted by diet changes, age, stress or medication, it can show up as loose stools, gas, itching or low energy. Restoring balance supports digestion and overall wellness.

Balanced digestion

Beneficial strains help break down food and absorb nutrients, which supports firmer stools and less gas.

Stronger immunity

A balanced microbiome supports the gut-based immune system that helps your dog stay resilient year-round.

Skin, coat & mood

The gut-skin connection means better digestive balance can show up as a softer coat and steadier energy.

Safety

Are Dog Probiotics Safe?

Research and veterinary guidance generally regard probiotics as safe and well tolerated in healthy dogs. Some dogs may have mild, temporary digestive changes when starting one. Dogs that are ill, immunocompromised, or on medication should be evaluated by a veterinarian first.

When to talk to your veterinarian

  • Before starting any new supplement, especially if your dog has a health condition or takes medication.
  • If digestive issues are severe, contain blood, or persist beyond a couple of days.
  • If you notice any unusual reaction after starting a probiotic.
