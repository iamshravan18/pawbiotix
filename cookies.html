Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings, and set your browser to refuse them. Some features may not work as intended if you disable certain cookies. Where required, we will ask for consent before setting non-essential cookies.

Third parties

Some cookies may be set by third-party services we use, such as analytics providers and the ClickBank affiliate network, under their own policies.
