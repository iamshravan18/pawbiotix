References & Sources

Our educational content on dog probiotics, the canine gut microbiome and digestive wellness is grounded in reputable veterinary organizations and peer-reviewed research. Sources are grouped below so you can verify anything for yourself.

Last reviewed: August 2026

Veterinary & pet-health organizations

  1. American Kennel Club (AKC). "How Probiotics Work in Every Stage of a Dog's Life." akc.org
  2. PetMD (vet-verified). "Probiotics for Dogs." petmd.com

Peer-reviewed research & journals

  1. Systematic review: "Clinical effect of probiotics in the prevention or treatment of gastrointestinal disease in dogs." PubMed Central (PMC). ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
  2. "Application of Probiotics in Cats and Dogs: Benefits and Mechanisms." Veterinary Sciences, MDPI (2025). mdpi.com

Research databases

  1. National Institutes of Health — PubMed Central (PMC): open-access full-text biomedical and life-sciences literature. pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
  2. PubMed (NIH / National Library of Medicine): index of biomedical citations, including canine probiotic and microbiome studies. pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
  3. MDPI: open-access publisher of peer-reviewed journals, including Veterinary Sciences and Animals. mdpi.com

How we use these sources

We separate two kinds of information. General canine-health education — how probiotics, prebiotics and the gut microbiome support digestion, stool quality and immune function — is checked against the organizations and studies above. PawBiotix product facts are drawn only from official product information; where a detail isn't publicly available, we omit it rather than estimate.
