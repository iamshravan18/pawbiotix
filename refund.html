PawBiotix Refund Policy

PawBiotix is backed by a 60-day money-back guarantee. If it isn't the right fit for your dog, here's exactly how to get your money back — no guesswork.

Last updated: August 2026

The 60-day money-back guarantee

Give PawBiotix an honest try for a full 60 days. Because the gut microbiome rebuilds gradually, this window gives your dog time to adjust while keeping your purchase risk-free. If you're not thrilled with the results — or you simply change your mind — you can request a refund of your purchase price within 60 days of your order. Orders are processed by the authorized retailer through ClickBank's secure checkout, which administers the guarantee.

Who's eligible

  • Your request is made within 60 days of your original purchase date.
  • The order was placed through the official website via ClickBank's secure checkout.
  • You can provide your order ID or receipt number and the email used at checkout.

How to request a refund — step by step

Reach out within 60 days

Contact PawBiotix support by email at contact@pawbiotix-product.com, or use ClickBank order self-service at clkbank.com. US customers can also call 1-800-390-6035.

Share your order details

Include your order ID or receipt number and the email you used at checkout. A quick note on why you're requesting a refund helps the team process it faster.

Follow the return instructions

Support will confirm whether a return is needed. If it is, they'll email you the current return address and simple instructions. Hold on to your bottles until you hear back.

Get your refund confirmed

Once approved, your refund is issued to your original payment method and you'll receive email confirmation. Your bank posts the credit shortly after.

Return instructions

Not every request requires you to mail anything back — support will tell you what's needed for your specific order. If a physical return is requested, pack the item securely, include your order ID inside the parcel, and use a tracked shipping service so the package can be confirmed as delivered.

Return address

To make sure your parcel reaches the correct facility, the current return address is provided by the support team when your refund is approved — please don't send anything back before you've received it. Addresses can change between fulfillment centers, so using the one confirmed for your order avoids lost packages and delays.

Return shipping responsibility

When a return is required, return shipping costs are generally the customer's responsibility unless support advises otherwise. We recommend a tracked, insured service and keeping your postage receipt until the refund is complete.

Refund processing & timing

After your refund is approved, it's credited back to the original payment method used at checkout. Processing on our side is prompt; the time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank or card network and is usually a few business days. If you don't see the credit after a reasonable period, contact support with your order ID and we'll help track it down.

Good to know

  • The guarantee covers your purchase price; any original shipping fee may be non-refundable depending on the order.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method only.
  • Always order through the official website so your purchase is covered by the guarantee — third-party marketplace purchases may not qualify.
