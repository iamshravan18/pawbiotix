PawBiotix Shipping Policy

Here's exactly what happens after you order PawBiotix dog probiotics — from order confirmation and tracking to delivery windows at home and abroad.

Last updated: August 2026

How the shipping process works

Once you complete the secure ClickBank checkout on the official website, your order is queued for fulfillment and prepared for dispatch. Orders are packed and handed to the carrier promptly, and you're kept in the loop by email at each key step so you always know where things stand.

Order confirmation

Immediately after checkout you'll receive an order confirmation email with your receipt and order details. Keep this email — your order ID is the fastest way for support to look things up if you have a question later.

Tracking email within 60 hours

When your order ships, you'll get a second email containing your tracking number, typically within 60 hours of placing the order. That link lets you follow the package from dispatch all the way to your door. If it hasn't arrived, check your spam or promotions folder before reaching out.

Shipping timeline & delivery expectations

Delivery time is the carrier's transit time after dispatch and varies by destination. US orders usually arrive within about a week, while international orders take a little longer and may pass through customs. The estimates below are typical working-day ranges (Monday–Friday, excluding holidays) and can shift during peak periods or due to carrier delays.

International shipping

PawBiotix ships to Canada, the UK and Ireland, and Australia and New Zealand in addition to the United States. International orders carry a flat $15.95 shipping fee and generally arrive within 10–15 working days once dispatched. Enter a complete, accurate address so the carrier can deliver without delay.

Customs & duties (international orders)

Packages crossing a border may be inspected by local customs, which can add a few days to the delivery estimate above — this step is handled by your country's authorities and is outside the carrier's control. Depending on where you live, import duties or taxes may apply and are the recipient's responsibility. If your tracking pauses at a customs facility, it's usually routine processing; contact support if it doesn't move for an extended period.

Quick tips for a smooth delivery

  • Enter a full street address where someone can receive the package.
  • Save your confirmation and tracking emails until the order arrives.
  • Review your shipping address carefully before confirming — changes may not be possible once a parcel is in transit.

Shipping FAQ

Where can I receive my order?

We ship to the United States, Canada, the UK and Ireland, and Australia and New Zealand. Provide a complete street address where the package can be received.

Can my billing and shipping address be different?

Yes. Your billing address needs to match your payment method, but the order can ship somewhere else. Just confirm the shipping address at checkout before you place the order.

How long will delivery take?

US orders typically arrive in about 5–7 working days. Canada, the UK and Ireland, and Australia and New Zealand usually take about 10–15 working days, plus any customs processing.

How much does shipping cost?

US shipping is free. International destinations have a flat $15.95 fee, and the 6-bottle package ships free within the US.

How do I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships, typically within 60 hours. Use that link to follow the package to delivery.

I entered the wrong shipping address — what now?

Contact support right away. If the order hasn't shipped, the address can usually be corrected; once it's in transit, changes may not be possible.

Who do I contact with other shipping questions?

Reach us through the channels on our contact page — email product support, use ClickBank order self-service, or call the toll-free (US) or international line.
